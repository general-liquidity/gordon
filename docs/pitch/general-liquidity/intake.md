# Intake: General Liquidity / Gordon
*Skill: startup-positioning | Source: docs/pitch/general-liquidity-company-brief-v2.md*

## Product
Gordon — the first agentic vibe trading product. Chat-first terminal that executes across crypto exchanges and stock brokers under a human-controlled six-mode permission model (auto / ask / strict / paper / observe / plan).

## Problem
Retail investors have access to markets but no agency over them. Institutions run fully systematized AI operations (quant systems, microstructure models, state-of-the-art economists); a retail user with a Robinhood + Coinbase account reacts to price movements with no infrastructure to turn a market view into a disciplined, monitored position. The asymmetry is not accidental — it is the product financial institutions sell.

## What customers do today instead
- **Single-venue retail AI** (Robinhood Cortex, Schwab AI, Public): research summaries only, no execution delegation, single market
- **DeFi copilots** (aixbt, Olas, Wayfinder, Velvet): crypto-only, no stocks, no broker, no personal finance
- **Robo-advisors** (Betterment, Wealthfront): static allocation, no thesis-driven execution
- **TradingView + manual execution**: research tool, no agent layer, purely manual
- **Spreadsheet/journal tracking**: self-built, no execution authority
- **Discord/X alpha groups + copying trades manually**: social signal, no automation, no risk gates
- **Platform AI assistants** (Apple Intelligence, Google Assistant): platform-aligned, no financial execution
- **Doing nothing / candlestick charts on phone**: overwhelmingly dominant status quo

## Customers
- Gen Z and younger millennials, 20–35, already have Robinhood + Coinbase accounts
- Crypto-native retail traders who feel the tool-vs-institution asymmetry acutely
- Self-directed investors who read market analysis but can't systematize execution
- Technical early adopters comfortable with terminal UIs (Claude Code, Cursor users)
- People who follow finance X/Twitter but have no way to act on their views with discipline
- Currently in friends-alpha phase (v0.9.0) — first cohort of invited operators

## Key differentiators (founder-stated)
- Only product spanning crypto + stocks with real execution authority
- Six permission modes enforced at every venue call (permissions travel with the agent)
- Plan-first: every trade previewed as structured diff before anything hits a venue
- Multi-venue: 9 crypto exchanges + 9 stock brokers + onchain (Solana, EVM, Polkadot, Base)
- Deep research + backtesting engine (Monte Carlo, walk-forward validation)
- Human-above-the-loop architecture, not human-in-the-loop

## Product ladder

3. Gordon Personal Finance OS (later) — full financial operating system across cards, banks, wallets, exchanges
