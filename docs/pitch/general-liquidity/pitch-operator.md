## Common Objections and Responses

### 1. "I'm worried it'll do something I didn't ask for."

This is exactly why the plan-first preview exists. Gordon does not execute a single order without showing you a complete structured preview first — exact positions, sizes, risk parameters, venue routing. You approve, modify, or reject. Until you approve, nothing happens. The permission modes are not just a toggle — they travel with Gordon through every agent action, at every venue call. Strict mode requires approval for each individual action. You define the trust level; Gordon operates within it.

### 2. "I've tried trading bots before. They broke."

Most trading bots break because they separate research from execution. You configure the bot, market conditions change, the bot doesn't adapt, and the loss is yours. Gordon is different in structure: it reasons through current conditions with every plan, not off a static config. When conditions change, the plan reflects that. And you're reviewing the plan before it executes, every time — so there's no "bot that went rogue" scenario. You're always the last decision point.

### 3. "I can just use ChatGPT + my broker's API."

You can, and a lot of people have tried. The typical result: ChatGPT gives you a trade idea in text, you manually execute it in a separate app, sizing is guesswork, there's no position monitoring, no reconciliation, and no memory between sessions. Gordon handles the full loop — scan, plan, preview, approve, execute, monitor, reconcile — in one context that persists across sessions. It's not a chatbot connected to an API. It's a purpose-built execution environment that happens to be chat-first.

### 4. "It seems expensive. TradingView is $15/month."

TradingView is a research and charting tool. It doesn't execute trades, doesn't manage positions, doesn't connect to multiple venues, and doesn't have an agent layer. The comparison is like comparing a calculator to an accountant. If you're managing $10,000+ in active positions, execution quality matters — even a 0.5% improvement in entry/exit timing covers the monthly cost. The free paper trading tier lets you verify the value before you pay anything.

### 5. "I don't want an AI touching my money autonomously."

Then don't use auto mode. Paper mode runs full simulations with no real capital. Observe mode watches your existing positions without touching them. Plan mode builds plans but doesn't execute anything. Ask mode gets your approval before every action. Strict mode is the most restrictive live option — every single action requires your explicit approval. Auto mode only activates when you choose it, within parameters you define. The product was designed for operators who don't want to give up control — because we talked to those operators, and they're right.
