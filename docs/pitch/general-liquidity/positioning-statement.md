## Freemium Positioning

**Free tier (`observe` + `paper` mode):**
*"Full research and paper trading — no live capital required. Learn what Gordon would have done before you risk a dollar."*
Free tier is COMPLETE for its scope: it delivers the research + planning + simulation value with zero capital risk. This is not a crippled demo. It's the right on-ramp for The Stuck Operator who has been burned before.

**Pro tier (live execution, 3 venues):**
*"When you're ready to go live — Gordon executes your thesis across crypto and stocks with every risk gate in place."*
Pro extends scope (live capital, real venues) — it does not "unlock" what Free withheld. The upgrade moment is natural: operator has paper-traded a strategy, seen it work, and wants to go live.

**Power tier (auto mode, all venues, backtesting):**
*"For operators who've validated their thesis and want full systematic execution — run strategies across all 18 venues with institutional-grade backtesting."*
