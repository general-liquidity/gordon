## Pricing Psychology Breakdown

### Gordon

**$0 Free Tier: Conviction-building without capital risk**
The free tier includes full research + paper trading. This is deliberate. The ICP has been burned before (failed DIY bots, copy trading losses). Getting them to trust a new system requires a zero-risk ramp. The value delivered at $0 (research depth + paper execution) must be genuinely useful — if the free tier feels like a trial nag, it will fail.

*Psychological signal:* "We're confident enough in the value that we give you the research engine for free. The paywall is about live capital access, not about gatekeeping information."

**$49 Pro: Crossing the live execution threshold**
$49/mo is below the "serious subscription" threshold for someone who trades regularly (equivalent to 1-2 commissions on a legacy broker or less than 0.1% on a $50k portfolio). The 3-venue limit creates a natural upgrade path for operators who connect to their first 3 venues and want more.

*Psychological signal:* "Commitment to the workflow, not a high-risk bet."

**$149 Power: The serious operator tier**
Auto mode (no confirmation required for every action) is the feature that serious operators will pay for once they trust the system. Backtesting at this tier makes sense — it's a research-depth feature that justifies the higher price. The $149 price point is competitive with Bloomberg's retail-adjacent products and well below any institutional terminal.

*Psychological signal:* "You've built conviction in the system. Now remove the friction."

**$299 Desk: Team / prop desk tier**
Multi-operator + shared context is the right feature set for small prop desks, family offices, or operator teams. $299/seat is well below any institutional tooling. The "shared context" feature is the key — it creates collaboration value that doesn't exist in any competitor product.

*Psychological signal:* "Infrastructure that scales with your team."

### Competitor Pricing Psychology

**3Commas $37–$119:** Priced as a bot tool, not a research-plus-execution platform. Operators who are paying $37/mo for 3Commas and seeing Gordon's $49 Pro tier face a direct trade-off. The switch is a "modest upgrade" not a "big jump" — this is favorable for Gordon's conversion from 3Commas users.

**Robinhood Cortex $5:** Priced as a feature bundled into a brokerage. This trains users to expect AI trading assistance to be nearly free. Gordon must demonstrate why $49 is a different product category, not a more expensive version of Robinhood Gold.

**TradingView $14.95–$59.95:** The ICP often has a TradingView subscription already. Gordon is not replacing TradingView's charting value. The positioning must be additive: "Gordon executes what you research on TradingView" rather than "Gordon replaces TradingView."

**Betterment/Wealthfront 0.25% AUM:** AUM-based pricing for a $50k portfolio = $125/yr = ~$10/mo. Appears cheaper. But the value is incomparable — robo-advisors are passive allocation, not operator-grade execution. The ICP who is comparing these is not Gordon's ICP.

## Pricing Whitespace Assessment

### Whitespace 1: The "Trust Ramp" is unoccupied
No competitor offers a free tier with genuine value (not just a trial). Gordon's free tier with full research + paper trading is structurally differentiated. The whitespace: operators who have been burned by copy trading or failed bots and are re-entering with caution. They need a zero-risk entry point. Gordon is the only product that provides this. [Estimate]

### Whitespace 2: Team / prop desk pricing at sub-enterprise rates
$299/seat is below the floor of institutional terminal pricing (Bloomberg Terminal is ~$2,000/mo/seat). The small prop desk and family office segment is significantly underserved by both consumer tools (too simple) and institutional tools (too expensive / require compliance infrastructure). Gordon's Desk tier occupies this gap. [Estimate]

### Whitespace 3: No per-trade or per-commission model
Every competitor either charges per AUM (robo-advisors), per subscription tier (3Commas, TradingView), or bundles into execution (Public.com via PFOF, Robinhood via brokerage). Gordon's clean SaaS subscription model avoids the conflict of interest inherent in PFOF (where the broker profits from routing, not from operator outcomes) and the growth penalty inherent in AUM % models. [Data]

### Whitespace 4: The $49–$149 gap is the right competitive zone
The $49 entry point is above Robinhood Gold ($5) and Robinhood Cortex — signaling a different product category. It is below 3Commas Pro ($99+) while offering more capability. It is below TradingView Premium ($59.95) while adding execution that TradingView doesn't have. The $49 position is the sweet spot: accessible, clearly not a feature bundle, clearly below the "serious trading tool" premium. [Estimate]

### Whitespace 5: Onchain + CEX + stocks at a single price
No competitor offers unified pricing for a stack that spans onchain, crypto exchanges, and stock brokers. DeFi copilots charge separately (or free/token-gated). Stock brokers charge separately. Crypto tools charge separately. Gordon's single subscription covering all three is structurally novel. [Data]

## Pricing Risk Assessment

**Risk: Robinhood Cortex anchors expectations at $5**
If the ICP's mental model is "AI trading assistance = $5/mo (Robinhood Gold)," Gordon's $49 must immediately communicate that it is a fundamentally different product. The onboarding and positioning must make the category difference visceral, not just verbal.

**Risk: 3Commas price similarity at low tiers**
3Commas Starter at ~$37/mo is close to Gordon's $49 Pro. An operator comparing the two on price alone will need clear demonstration of the plan-preview and permission model advantages to justify the premium.

**Risk: Free tier cannibalization**
A free tier with genuine research value might reduce urgency to upgrade to Pro if the ICP's primary use case is research rather than execution. The free-to-paid conversion strategy must create moments where the paper trading experience surfaces its limits and makes live execution feel like the obvious next step.
