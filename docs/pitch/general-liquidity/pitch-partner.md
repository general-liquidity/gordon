## 1-Page Partner Brief

### Who Gordon Is

Gordon is the agentic trading terminal for retail operators — the first product that executes across crypto exchanges, stock brokers, and onchain with a plan-first permission model that shows the operator exactly what will happen before any order fires.

Currently in friends-alpha (v0.9.0) with a public launch planned for April 2026. Infrastructure covers 9 crypto exchanges, 9 stock brokers, and onchain (Solana, EVM, Polkadot, Base).

### Who the Operators Are

Gordon's primary operator profile is "The Stuck Operator" — age 22–34, manages $5k–$75k actively, has accounts at multiple brokerages and exchanges, is technically comfortable (uses Claude Code, Cursor, or has built Alpaca/CCXT integrations), and has the market conviction but lacks the execution infrastructure to act on it systematically.

This is the highest-engagement retail segment: operators who generate real order flow, have multiple accounts, understand advanced order types, and are actively looking for better infrastructure. They are not passive investors — they are systematic traders who have already demonstrated willingness to pay for tooling (Cursor, Claude Code, TradingView subscriptions).

Key indicators:
- Multiple live brokerage and exchange accounts
- History of attempting to build trading automation
- Follows both equities and crypto markets simultaneously
- CLI-comfortable; willing to pay $49–$299/month for execution quality

### What Integration Would Unlock

For an exchange or brokerage partner, Gordon integration means:
- A dedicated, high-engagement distribution channel into the Stuck Operator segment — the cohort most likely to generate consistent order flow
- Your API and liquidity surfaced at the moment of operator intent, when the thesis is formed and the plan is approved
- Preferential venue routing visibility — Gordon shows operators the plan including venue selection; partner venues can be surfaced with context (spread, liquidity, fee tier)
- Operators who stay: Gordon's persistent context means operators build their trading history on the platform, making them sticky to the venues they use through Gordon
- Data flywheel participation: operators who use Gordon through your venue generate compounding context that improves execution quality over time — creating a virtuous cycle for the partner venue

## Value Proposition for the Partner

Gordon is not asking for marketing cooperation. This is a distribution and flow conversation.

**What Gordon brings to a partner:**

1. **High-intent operator flow.** Gordon operators are systematic traders executing thesis-driven positions. They generate multiple trades per week, across multiple instruments. This is not casual retail — it is the operator segment with highest lifetime order flow value.

2. **Superior execution context.** Gordon operators approve a structured plan before execution. They know their position size, venue, and risk parameters before the order fires. This means cleaner order flow with lower chargeback and dispute rates relative to emotional retail flow.

3. **First-mover category alignment.** Agentic trading is crystallizing as a category in Q1-Q2 2026 (Public.com launched as "the world's first agentic brokerage" March 31; NickAI launched March 12; 3Commas QuantPilot launched April 2). Partners who integrate early establish preferred routing relationships before the category scales. The Santander/Mastercard live AI-executed regulated financial transaction completed in March 2026 signals institutional readiness for this category.

4. **Multi-venue operators.** Gordon operators connect multiple accounts. A partner who integrates becomes one of several accessible venues — and operators naturally allocate flow toward venues that offer the best execution, fee transparency, and product support. Gordon surfaces that comparison at the moment of plan review.

5. **Branded presence in the operator workflow.** Gordon's plan preview explicitly shows venue routing. Partners are visible by name in the execution plan the operator approves. This is persistent, high-intent brand exposure — not banner advertising.

**The broader category argument:**

60–75% of US equity volume is algorithmic. Only 7–13% of retail traders achieve positive P&L vs. 37%+ for AI agents (Polymarket 2026 data). The gap is documented and growing. Venues and brokerages that integrate with agentic execution infrastructure now are positioning for the operator segment that will drive the next five years of retail flow.
