Good morning! Here's your trading brief:

## Overnight Recap
- What happened while I was away? Major moves, news, events
- How did my open positions perform overnight?

## Today's Key Levels
- For each position I hold: current price vs stop loss vs take profit
- Any positions approaching stop loss or take profit?

## Market Outlook
- Current market regime (trending up, down, ranging?)
- Key economic events today that could move markets

## Today's Watchlist
- Top 3 symbols showing interesting setups right now
- Quick technical read on each (bullish/bearish/neutral)

## Action Items
- Any orders I should adjust?
- Any positions I should close?
- Any new opportunities worth exploring?

Keep it concise — I want to scan this in 2 minutes.
