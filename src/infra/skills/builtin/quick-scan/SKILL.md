Give me a fast market snapshot:

1. **Top movers**: Get trending tokens and biggest gainers/losers in the last 24h
2. **Volume leaders**: Show the highest volume pairs right now
3. **Quick setups**: Scan for any obvious technical setups (breakouts, oversold bounces)

Keep it brief — bullet points, no deep analysis. Focus on what's actionable RIGHT NOW.

If {market} is "stocks", check broker positions and market indices instead of crypto.
