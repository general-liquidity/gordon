Run a comprehensive risk check on my current portfolio:

1. **Positions**: List all open positions with current P&L
2. **Concentration**: Am I over-exposed to any single asset?
3. **Drawdown**: How far am I from my daily loss limit? Max drawdown?
4. **Correlation**: Are my positions correlated (all moving together)?
5. **Open orders**: Any stop losses missing? Take profits set?
6. **Recommendations**: What should I adjust to reduce risk?

Be direct. If something is dangerous, say so clearly.
